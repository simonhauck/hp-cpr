# Guided Rhythmusanalyse with automatic CPR resume

**Status:** accepted

The Analysephase exposed four actions plus a result summary and a shock status row, and the Zeitnehmer had to record the result and then tap `CPR fortsetzen`. A separately recorded Schock froze the compression timer as a `Schockpause`. We replace that with a guided wizard: the Zeitnehmer records the Rhythmusart and, for a shockable rhythm, the rhythm and whether a shock was delivered; the app then starts the next Druckphase immediately, or enters ROSC. The Analysephase footer keeps only `Überspringen` and `Ergebnis erfassen`; exceptions move to a secondary `Aktionen` entry point. This matches the guideline rule to resume compressions immediately after a shock, removes a redundant confirmation, and makes the next action obvious. The running case also gains a phase strip with the current action and the shock count, and a `Weitere Aktionen` sheet for measurements, notes, manual shock, and `DruckphaseNeustart`.

**Considered options:** Keeping the explicit `CPR fortsetzen` was rejected because it adds a tap and contradicts immediate CPR after a shock. Keeping `Schock` as a permanently visible one-tap action was rejected because it clutters the phase and duplicates the guided flow. Keeping the quick shock undo was rejected in favour of a single correction surface in the log. Keeping `Schockpause` was rejected because a manual shock is now record-only and the guided flow always resumes CPR.

**Consequences:** `Schockpause` leaves the active domain and glossary. A manual `Schock` records the event without pausing the timer; the rare case where the cycle must restart is covered by the explicit `DruckphaseNeustart`. The result note and the skip reason are removed from the active flow; notes remain a general `Notiz`. Quick shock correction and undo are removed; log entry editing is a follow-up and must ship before production. There is no production deployment yet, so no legacy state migration is required.
